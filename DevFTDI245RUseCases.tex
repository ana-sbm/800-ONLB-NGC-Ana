\section{Monitor Sensor Status}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{L{3.5cm}Y}

\bottomrule\endlastfoot
\textbf{Identifier} & \UseCaseID{UC-005}\\
\textbf{Goal} & Enable the operator to interpret current sensor values and their availability, validity, and age when deciding whether to use a reading.\\
\textbf{Primary actor} & \hyperref[Stakeholder:Operator]{operator}.\\
\textbf{Linked stories} & \UserStoryRef{US-004}, \UserStoryRef{US-007}. \UserStoryRef{US-006} applies across supported platforms.\\
\textbf{Mapped requirements} & \RequirementRef{REQ-F-004}, \RequirementRef{REQ-F-006}, \RequirementRef{REQ-F-007}, \RequirementRef{REQ-I-003}, \RequirementRef{REQ-Q-006}, \RequirementRef{REQ-Q-007}, \RequirementRef{REQ-Q-008}, \RequirementRef{REQ-F-010}, \RequirementRef{REQ-I-004}. All are proposed candidates.\\
\textbf{Mapped tests} & TBD; owner: A. Crone; decision date: TBD.\\
\textbf{Precondition} & The host subsystem is running and the operator can access its published sensor information. A current sample need not exist.\\
\textbf{Trigger} & The operator requests sensor information, or the subsystem detects a change in sample or sensor status.\\
\textbf{Main flow 1} & Determine sensor availability using the agreed detection rules.\\
\textbf{Main flow 2} & Obtain the latest published sample and its validity information, when present.\\
\textbf{Main flow 3} & Determine sample age using the agreed timestamp semantics.\\
\textbf{Main flow 4} & Expose the current value, availability, validity, and age through the agreed operator interaction mechanism.\\
\textbf{Main flow 5} & The operator reviews that information and decides whether to use the reading.\\
\textbf{Alternate: no sample} & Indicate that no reading is available.\\
\textbf{Alternate: invalid} & Identify the invalid condition.\\
\textbf{Alternate: aging} & Expose age and identify staleness according to the agreed rule.\\
\textbf{Alternate: disconnect} & Indicate unavailability and distinguish any retained reading from newly acquired data.\\
\textbf{Alternate: recovery} & Update availability and sample information after the agreed recovery conditions are met.\\
\textbf{Postcondition} & The operator can distinguish an available, valid, current reading from an invalid, aging, stale, unavailable, or absent reading. The operator retains responsibility for the use decision.\\
\textbf{Open decisions} & TBD: operator interaction mechanism, availability detection rules, stale-data threshold, age calculation, and recovery criteria. Owner: A. Crone; decision date: TBD.\\
\end{tabularx}
